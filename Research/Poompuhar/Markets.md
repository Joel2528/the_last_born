# Poompuhar Markets: Naalangadi & Allangadi

## Historical Reference

- **Naalangadi**: The Day Market active from sunrise to sunset, selling silk, spices, pearls, gold, and agricultural produce.
- **Allangadi**: The Night Market active from dusk till late night, famous for music, street performers, and food stalls.

---

## Game Implementation

- Dynamic day/night cycle script (`GameManager.cs`) shifts NPC schedules between Naalangadi and Allangadi.
- Market stalls supply items for the `InventorySystem.cs`.
