# Poompuhar City Layout & Urban Planning

## Historical Context

Poompuhar (Kaveripoompattinam) was divided into two distinct urban sectors as described in the *Pattinappalai* and *Silappathikaram*:

1. **Maruvurpakkam**: The coastal harbour town populated by foreign merchants, sailors, weavers, and dock workers.
2. **Pattinapakkam**: The inland royal city containing palaces, noble residences, and administrative quarters.
3. **Naalangadi & Allangadi**: The central open-air marketplace connecting both sectors.
