## Primary Research Domains

1. **Poompuhar (Kaveripoompattinam)**
   - Ancient Chola port city at the mouth of the Kaveri river.
   - Known for international trade with Rome, Greece, and Southeast Asia.
   - Submerged during ancient tsunami/sea-level rise events.

2. **Silappathikaram (Epic of the Ankle Bracelet)**
   - Author: Prince Elango Adigal.
   - Details city life, merchant guilds, festivals (*Indra Vizha*), and social hierarchy in Poompuhar.

3. **Manimekalai**
   - Author: Chithalai Chathanar.
   - Sequel to Silappathikaram, detailing the submergence of Poompuhar under the ocean by Goddess Manimekalai.

4. **Kumari Kandam**
   - Legendary continent south of Kanyakumari described in Tamil literature.
   - Ancient academies (Sangams) and lost civilizational knowledge.
