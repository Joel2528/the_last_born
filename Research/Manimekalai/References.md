# Manimekalai & The Submergence Legend

## Cataclysmic Lore

In the epic *Manimekalai*, Poompuhar is swallowed by the ocean when the annual festival for Goddess Manimekalai is neglected.

- **Game Context**: Provides the mythic explanation for the flooded ruins level in *The Last Born*.
- **Submerged Level**: The player dives into underwater temples (*Kumari Kandam / Submerged Poompuhar*) to retrieve lost ancient technological artifacts.
