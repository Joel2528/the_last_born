# Silappathikaram Literary References

## Summary for Game Narrative

*Silappathikaram* (The Tale of an Ankle Bracelet) written by Ilango Adigal serves as the foundational narrative source for *The Last Born*.

- **Key Characters & NPCs**: Kovalan, Kannagi, Madhavi, King Senguttuvan.
- **Lore Objects**: The Rubied Ankle Bracelet (*Silambu*), Ancient Chola Seal, Sangam Scrolls.
- **Environmental Scenes**: Indra Vizha festival grounds, dance pavilions, sea coast temple.
