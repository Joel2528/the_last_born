🏛️ Historical World Building

Meticulously reconstructs ancient Tamil coastal ports (*Maruvurpakkam*) and inland royal quarters (*Pattinapakkam*) based on archaeological blueprints and ancient epics.

🌊 Submerged Civilization

Explore the legend of Kumari Kandam and underwater ruins with dynamic water physics, ancient hydraulic puzzles, and submerged codex recovery.

⚔️ Combat & Quest Systems

Engage in fluid third-person combat using traditional *Silambam* staff techniques and solve questlines tied directly to Sangam historical figures.
