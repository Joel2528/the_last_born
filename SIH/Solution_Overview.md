# Smart India Hackathon 2026 - Solution Overview

## Project Name: THE LAST BORN
### Team Astute

---

## 1. Problem Statement Alignment
- **Problem Statement ID**: SIH2026-GAME-001
- **Theme**: Toys & Games / Preserving Cultural Heritage through Digital Gaming
- **Objective**: Develop an engaging 3D digital game that accurately represents Indian history, literature, and architectural heritage while providing high-grade entertainment.

---

## 2. Technical & Cultural Innovation
- **Authentic Sangam Literature Integration**: Converts epics like *Silappathikaram* and *Manimekalai* into interactive gameplay quests.
- **Architectural Reconstruction**: 3D models built directly from archaeological survey blueprints of Poompuhar.
- **Interactive Cultural Codex**: In-game encyclopedic system explaining ancient maritime routes, trade goods, and scripts.
- **Modular Unity C# Architecture**: Scalable, decoupled state management suitable for expansion.

---

## 3. Impact & Scalability
- Educational adoption potential in schools and cultural museums.
- Scalable level expansion covering other ancient Indian dynasties (Pallavas, Cheras, Pandyas, Guptas).
