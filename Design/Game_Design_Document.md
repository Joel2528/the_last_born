# Game Design Document (GDD)

# THE LAST BORN

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Title**: The Last Born
- **Genre**: 3D Historical-Fantasy Action Adventure
- **Target Platform**: PC / Console / WebGL Prototype
- **Target Audience**: Fans of historical fiction, open-world action-adventure (e.g. Assassin's Creed, Tomb Raider), and Indian heritage enthusiasts.

---

## 2. Narrative & Lore
You play as **Varman**, a young navigator trained in ancient Tamil maritime astronomy. When a mysterious relic resurfaces from the depths of the ocean near Poompuhar, Varman uncovers a lost lineage tied to the legendary continent of Kumari Kandam. He must navigate political intrigue, hostile invaders, and ancient environmental puzzles to stop a cataclysmic flood.

---

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Explore**: Traverse 3D historic environments (Poompuhar docks, markets, temples, underwater ruins).
2. **Interact**: Engage with historical NPCs, accept quests, gather codex entries.
3. **Solve**: Decode astronomical and hydraulic puzzles derived from ancient architecture.
4. **Combat**: Engage guards and mercenaries using traditional weapons (*Silambam* staff, *Urumi* flexible sword).
5. **Unlock**: Upgrade equipment, unlock ancient Tamil inscriptions, progress the story.

---

## 4. Key Level Environments
- **Level 1: Poompuhar Harbour (Day)** - Tutorial, movement, NPC interaction, marketplace.
- **Level 2: Chola Palace & Secret Catacombs** - Stealth, puzzle solving, document recovery.
- **Level 3: Submerged City of Kumari Kandam** - Underwater exploration, ancient machinery, boss encounter.
